Module · Target Guide

Each morning, every employee knows whom to call

  1. Measures the gap: target minus achieved, and what’s needed each working day left.
  2. Finds and values opportunities in their own customers and leads: likely amount × chance of success.
  3. Sends a daily list of 10–15 customers, each with the reason, the allowed offer and a message.
  4. Helps them act: one tap to call, send an approved WhatsApp message, or ask the AI to call.
  5. Checks results in the gold loan system and updates tomorrow’s plan.
  6. Escalates: behind for a week? The branch manager gets the reason and a suggested fix.

Where the business comes from

Eight sources the Target Guide works every day

Renewals due

Own customers due in the next 7–30 days, renewed in your app or at the branch.

Top-ups

Pledged gold is worth more after a rate rise, within the RBI LTV cap. Triggered automatically.

Overdue interest

Protects loan quality, and often leads to a renewal.

Latent customers

Closed a loan in the last 3–12 months and not returned: invite them back.

Released gold

Released gold recently: a natural moment for a new loan.

Takeovers

Gold pledged at another lender, offered better value per gram.

New leads

Walk-ins, phone, app, WhatsApp, referrals and local campaigns, by area.

Cross-sell

Gold customers for your other loans (MSME, home, microfinance), and the reverse.

The plan picks the mix that closes the gap with the fewest, likeliest contacts: usually renewals first, then top-ups, win-backs and new leads.

Module · Appraisal & Incentives

Fair appraisals, with the reasons written down

Fair peer groups: compared with the same role at similar branches, by age, size and location.
Consistency counts: a steady quarter scores better than one big month.
Quality can cap the rating: high overdue, auctions or audit findings limit it.
Transfers and leave handled: scores split by time at each branch; leave lowers the target.
Managers decide: they can override with a reason, and the override is recorded.
