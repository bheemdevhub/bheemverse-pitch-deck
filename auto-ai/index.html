Module · Target Guide

Each morning, every consultant knows whom to call

  1. Measures the gap: bookings and retail target minus achieved, and what’s needed each day left.
  2. Finds and ranks opportunities in their own enquiries and customers: chance to book × value.
  3. Sends a daily list of 10–15 customers, each with the reason, the approved offer and a message.
  4. Helps them act: one tap to call, send an approved WhatsApp message, or ask the AI to call.
  5. Checks results in your DMS and updates tomorrow’s plan.
  6. Escalates: behind for a week? The outlet manager gets the reason and a suggested fix.
